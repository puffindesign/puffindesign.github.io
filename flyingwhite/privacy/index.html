Privacy Policy

Flying White · Last updated 28 September 2026

Flying White collects no personal data. What you write stays on your device and in your own iCloud — we never see it.

What stays with you

  • Your works. Every poem you write is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs through your own private iCloud storage so it appears on your other devices. That storage belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot read it, and nothing in a work identifies you.
  • Your brush strokes. The raw touch and pencil data from each stroke is used only in memory to draw the ink, then discarded. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Preferences are kept on the device and synced through your own iCloud, the same way.

If you sign out of iCloud or turn off iCloud for Flying White, iOS removes the synced copies from that device. Works that finished syncing come back when you sign in again with the same Apple Account.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the image of your work is made on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. It carries no location or other hidden data, and it goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, no card numbers and no billing address.

To know whether you have unlocked all poems, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription history under a random ID created for this app. That ID is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or any other identity, and we never attach any personal details to it. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Flying White contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. It does not track you across apps or websites, so it never asks for tracking permission. Because nothing is tracked, browser “Do Not Track” signals have nothing to change.

Children

Flying White is not directed at children. It has no accounts, no chat, no user-to-user content and no social features, and it collects nothing that could identify anyone.

Your rights

Your works live on your devices and in your iCloud, so they are yours to remove: deleting the app removes them from that device, and you can erase the app’s iCloud data in iOS Settings under your name, then iCloud. The only record we can reach is the anonymous purchase history; if you want it erased, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version of Flying White collects anything new, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will tell you when.
