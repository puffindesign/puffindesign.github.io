Privacy Policy

Suisheng (working name) · Last updated 28 September 2026

Suisheng collects no personal data. Your scripts, your voice and your recordings never leave your device. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your scripts. Each script is stored as a file on your device. We never receive it.
  • Your voice. While you read, speech recognition runs on your device using Apple’s built-in speech framework. The words it hears are kept in memory only long enough to follow your place in the script. They are never written to storage, never logged and never sent anywhere.
  • Your recordings. Audio and video are saved only when you choose to record a take, and they stay on the device. A take goes to your photo library only when you tap to save it there.
  • Your settings. Display and reading preferences stay on the device.

Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — so the script can follow your voice.
  • Camera — only if you record video of yourself reading.
  • Photos (add only) — only to save a take you choose to keep. We cannot read your photo library.

You can decline any of these. Without the microphone, the script still scrolls by hand or at a steady pace.

Network use

The first time you use a language, iOS may download its speech model from Apple. That download is managed by Apple, not by us, and none of your content is part of it. After that, reading and recording work fully offline.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, no card numbers and no billing address.

To confirm that your purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat. It receives your purchase history — what was bought and when — together with a random identifier created by the app. That identifier is not your Apple ID, is not linked to your name or email, and is not used to track you. This is the only data that leaves your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share crash reports with app developers, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to us; you control that setting, not us.

Children

Suisheng is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone. It has no chat, no user-to-user content and no social features.

Your rights

Because your content never reaches us, there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. Deleting the app removes your scripts, settings and any recordings you have not saved to Photos.

Changes

If a future version collects anything new, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will tell you when.
