Privacy Policy

WallSitPal · Last updated 13 September 2026

You don’t need an account to use WallSitPal, and it has no server of its own. Your holds stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Data reaches a third party only when you choose to watch an ad or make a purchase.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Angle readings. The motion sensor reading that tells whether your thigh is in zone is processed on the device. No camera is used.
  • Your holds. For each hold the app keeps the date, the target and actual time, the time spent in zone, a steadiness value, and a one-point-per-second angle curve.
  • Settings and reminders. Preferences stay on the device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the iPhone itself.
  • A day of Plus from an ad. When you earn one, its expiry time is stored only on this iPhone.

What syncs through your iCloud

If iCloud is on for WallSitPal, your holds sync to your other devices through your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot read it, and it is not shared with anyone.

Apple Health

If you allow it, completed sessions are saved to Apple Health as workouts. WallSitPal only writes to Apple Health; it never reads your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties.

Ads

WallSitPal shows an ad only when you tap to watch one in exchange for today’s Plus plan. Nothing plays on its own, and there are no ads during or right after a hold. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When you watch one, Google may receive:

  • your device’s advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, from which a general location may be estimated;
  • information about the ad and how you interacted with it, and diagnostic data about the ad software.

Ad requests never contain your holds, your angle readings or any Health data. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still get the same reward. Every ad carries an AdChoices ⓘ icon where you can see why it was shown and report it. Google explains its use of this data at policies.google.com.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details, card numbers or billing address. WallSitPal uses RevenueCat to confirm whether Plus is active; RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app identifier, not your name or email. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Analytics

WallSitPal contains no analytics SDK of its own. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass along anonymous crash reports.

Consumer health data

Under some US state laws, including Washington’s My Health My Data Act, your holds and the workouts saved to Apple Health can count as consumer health data. We use them only to provide the app’s features. We never sell them, and we never share them with advertisers or data brokers.

You stay in control of this data. Deleting WallSitPal removes what is on your iPhone; you can remove its iCloud data in your iPhone’s iCloud settings, and delete its workouts in the Health app. If you want help with any request about this data, write to us.

Children

WallSitPal is not directed to children under 13. It has no chat, no user-to-user content and no social features.
