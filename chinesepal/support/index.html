ChinesePal Support

ChinesePal · Write Chinese, stroke by stroke

Need a hand? Write to contact@puffindesign.ca. A real person reads every message, usually within two working days.

Purchases

  • Restore a purchase. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose ChinesePal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds. Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Free words and review

  • Daily new words. All 11,092 words are open to everyone. For your first 7 days, new words are unlimited; after that, free accounts learn 20 new words a day. Watch a rewarded video to add 10 more, as often as you like.
  • Review. Reviewing words you have already studied is always unlimited and free.

Your progress

  • Moving to a new device. Progress syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, open ChinesePal, and give it a moment to sync.
  • Handwriting. Strokes are checked on the device and never uploaded.
