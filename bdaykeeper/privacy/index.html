Privacy Policy

B-Day Keeper · Last updated 28 September 2026

B-Day Keeper has no accounts and no servers of its own. The people you keep, their dates, your gift notes and everything else you write stay on your device and in your own iCloud. We never receive them. The only data that leaves the device is listed below.

What stays with you

  • Your big days. Names, relationships, dates, photos, gifts and notes are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. We cannot see it.
  • Your contacts. When you pick people from Contacts, the app reads names and dates on the device and copies only what you choose into your big days. Your address book is never uploaded, and you can use the app without granting access.
  • Reminders. Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.
  • Calendar, widgets and Search. If you turn on the calendar option, the app writes your big days into a calendar on your device. Widgets and Spotlight search read from the same data on the device.
  • Privacy Lock. Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS itself. The app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Your settings. Reminder time, appearance and every other preference stay on the device.

Purchases

B-Day Keeper is free to start. Unlocking more big days is a one-time purchase or a yearly or monthly subscription, handled entirely by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active: it receives the App Store purchase record and a random ID created by the app, never your name, email or card details. We receive no payment details, no card numbers and no billing address. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to count a few anonymous events — for example that a big day was added, that a reminder was delivered or opened, or that a purchase was completed — so we can tell whether the app does its job. These events never contain names, dates, gifts, notes or anything else you write. Firebase uses an app-specific device identifier (not the advertising identifier) and basic device information. Advertising personalisation is switched off, the app never asks for permission to track you, and there are no ads. You can stop this at any time with Send anonymous usage data in Settings.

Crashes

Crash reports are collected by iOS. They reach us only through Apple, and only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Children

B-Day Keeper is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone. It has no chat, no user-to-user content and no social features.

Your rights

We hold no data that identifies you, so there is nothing of yours on our side to access or erase. Deleting a big day in the app removes it from your device and your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and, if you like, its data under iCloud settings on your device. For the anonymous statistics described above, or anything else, write to us and we will help.

Changes

If a future version of B-Day Keeper collects anything new, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will tell you when.
