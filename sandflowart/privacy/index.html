Privacy Policy

SandFlowArt · Last updated 30 August 2026

SandFlowArt collects no personal data. Your sand paintings never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Your shots. Press the shutter and the image is written to your own photo library. It is never uploaded to us, and we cannot see it.
  • Your settings. Sounds, haptics and everything you tune stay on the device.

What syncs, if you sign in

If you sign in to sync, the worlds you make — the colour and grain of each kind of sand, the liquid, the air — are stored so they follow you to your other devices. That is a short list of numbers and colour values. No images, no names, no contacts.

You can stop syncing at any time, and deleting a world removes it from the sync store.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, no card numbers and no billing address — only whether your subscription is currently active.

Ads

SandFlowArt shows an ad only when you tap to watch one in exchange for a Pro Pass. Nothing plays on its own, and there are no ads anywhere in the canvas. When you choose to watch one, the ad provider may use your device's advertising identifier to select it, subject to the tracking permission you granted iOS. If you decline tracking, you still get the pass.

Analytics

We collect anonymous usage statistics — which features are used, how the app performs, and whether it crashes — to improve SandFlowArt. This data is not linked to you, is never used for advertising, and contains nothing you create. You cannot be identified from it, and we do not try.

Children

SandFlowArt is safe for all ages and collects nothing that could identify a child. It has no chat, no user-to-user content and no social features.

Your rights

Because we hold almost nothing, there is little to request. If you have synced worlds and want them erased, write to us and we will delete them.

Changes

If a future version of SandFlowArt collects anything new, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will tell you when.
