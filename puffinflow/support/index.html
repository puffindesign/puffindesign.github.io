Puffin Flow Support

Puffin Flow · Boards with real milestones, for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Need a hand? Write to contact@puffindesign.ca. A real person reads every message, usually within two working days.

Purchases

  • Restore a purchase. Open App Settings in Puffin Flow (⌘, on Mac), choose Upgrade to Pro, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad.
  • Cancel a subscription. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Puffin Flow. On Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds. Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sync and sharing

  • Sync between devices. Puffin Flow syncs through your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and make sure iCloud Drive is on. There is no Puffin Flow account and no Puffin Flow server.
  • Inviting people to a Space. Sharing uses Apple’s standard iCloud sharing. The people you invite need an Apple Account; joining a shared Space is free for them, always.
  • Without iCloud. Everything keeps working on the device. Your boards simply stay on that one device until you sign in.

Deleted something by mistake?

Deleted cards and Spaces stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it from the Spaces list, or from a board’s menu, and restore what you need.

When you write

Please tell us your device model, the system version (iOS, iPadOS or macOS) and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps.
